This Week is the Worst on Record

The account hit 0.66x ROAS this week, the lowest reading since the account launched. The account spent $11,267 and generated $7,457 back. That is a net loss of approximately $3,810 before product costs. Two ads that were driving the majority of last week's revenue collapsed simultaneously: Good Shepherd Story Video went from 7.06x and 2 purchases to zero revenue across $846 in spend, and Eden Video C1 dropped from 4.82x and 3 purchases to 0.52x. A Site Checkout Problem is Compounding the Ad Issues

This is not purely a creative problem. The data shows the funnel is breaking at checkout level across multiple ads. Good Shepherd Story Video had 23 ATCs and 4 checkouts this week with zero purchases. Eden Video C1 had 27 ATCs and 6 checkouts but only 1 purchase. These conversion rates, 0% and 17% respectively at checkout, point to something happening between checkout initiation and purchase completion on the site. Oscar flagged checkout issues in the April 16 call. Clarity is the immediate diagnostic tool. This has to be investigated in parallel with the ad-level fixes.

Critical Diagnosis

The Checkout Problem: Intent Is There, Purchases Are Not

The account generated approximately 130 ATCs and 34 checkouts this week but only 13 purchases. A 38% checkout-to-purchase rate versus an expected 55-65% for e-commerce. Multiple ads show 0% conversion at the final step. This is a site issue, not an ad issue.

What
Good Shepherd Story Video had 23 ATCs and 4 checkout initiations with zero purchases. Eden Video C1 had 27 ATCs and 6 checkouts with only 1 purchase. Together those two ads represent $2,082 in spend, 50 ATCs, 10 checkouts, and just 1 purchase. Industry benchmark for checkout-to-purchase is 55-65%. The account is at 38% overall and certain products are at 0%.
Why
When checkout-to-purchase rate drops to 0% on specific ads and not others, the problem is almost always product-specific. The Good Shepherd and Eden products are likely experiencing a checkout block. This could be a Shopify glitch on a specific variant, a pricing configuration issue after Oscar's planned change to remove low-AOV options, or a payment processing failure on certain SKUs. This is a site-side issue the ads cannot fix.
Fix
Install Clarity immediately and check session recordings for users who initiated checkout on Good Shepherd and Eden products this week. Place a test order on both products through the same funnel path a customer would follow from the ad. Check Shopify's abandoned checkout report for this week specifically. If the pricing or SKU change was made mid-week, check whether it coincides with the drop in conversion. This is the single highest-impact action available to the account right now and it is not in Ads Manager.
