The Discipline Worked, This Is The Recovery The Plan Was Built To Produce

Last week's deck set the job plainly, protect a clean 3x base through June. One week on the account is at 3.13x, and it got there the right way, by taking spend down 35% rather than chasing revenue with budget. Daily spend fell from $1,221 to $789, revenue still rose 70%, and CPA dropped back under breakeven to $368. That is the post promo cuts and the ad level pauses landing exactly as intended, the leaking spend came out and the budget concentrated onto the units that convert. The account is now profitable again on a much lighter, much cleaner spend base, which is the platform the rest of June builds on.

Quality Of Demand Stepped Up, The Placement Cleanup Is Visible In The Numbers

The placement work from the call, locking delivery to Feed, Stories and Reels and removing Meta Shops, Marketplace and Audience Network, shows up directly. Link CTR rose from 1.64% to 2.16% and AOV nearly doubled to $1,153, so the traffic that remains is higher intent and higher value. The phantom Evergreen orders from last week, the ten dollar Last Supper purchases happening inside Meta Shops rather than on site, are gone now that Shop is off. Cleaner placements, fewer junk conversions, better basket value. Detail on slide 5.

TLIMS And Eden Carried The Recovery, Cold And Without Any Offer

TLIMS returned 5.40x on $2,605 for $14,071 and 11 purchases, more than 80% of account revenue. Eden Static is the engine inside it, 7.88x on $1,351 for $10,641 and 8 purchases. The point that matters, Eden did this cold with no promo behind it, after sitting at 0.18x in the trough week. Evergreen also came back from the phantom 0.01x week to 1.27x with real orders, led by the Eden Refresh Static at 5.04x, now that the Last Supper Meta Shops leak is closed.

Fine Art Softened, This Is The Constraint That Rotated In This Week

As TLIMS recovered, the Fine Art Value Opt campaign slipped to 1.24x from 2.58x. The Combined Grid, the steady carrier for three weeks, dropped to 0.46x on $1,228 with 38 carts and 1 purchase, the same cart to purchase pattern we tracked account wide last week, now isolated to the Fine Art lane. One bright spot held it up, a Fine Art Carousel variant returned 9.07x on $150. The read is that Fine Art creative is fatiguing and needs the refresh, which lines up perfectly with the sandbox testing plan on slide 7.

The Post Click Constraint From Last Week Has Substantially Cleared

Last Monday's read named the bottleneck as the path from cart to completed purchase, with checkout to purchase sitting at 13.8%. This week it is 36.6%, the single biggest funnel move of the recovery. Two things drove it. The Meta Shops cleanup removed the ten dollar Last Supper orders that were never real website conversions, and the placement lock onto Feed, Stories and Reels routed higher intent traffic to the site. Fewer checkouts overall, but a far higher share of them complete and at a much higher basket value. This is quality of demand improving, not just volume returning.

What This Means For The Eden Landing Page

The funnel improving does not retire the landing page, it sharpens its purpose. The checkout step is healthier, the next lever is the product page itself, turning more of Eden's cold clicks into carts and protecting the gains as traffic scales. With the account clean and Eden converting cold at 7.88x, the A and B test now runs on a trustworthy baseline. The page becomes an upside lever on the proven anchor rather than a repair, and a confirmed lift is what underwrites stepping budgets back up later in June.

This Is The Proof That The Cuts Improved The Account, Not Just Shrank It

The worry with any budget reduction is that revenue falls with it. The opposite happened. Spend came down 35% and revenue rose 70%, because the spend that came out was the spend that was leaking, the Last Supper video, the fatigued legacy units, the offer dependent creative with no offer left. What remained is the proven core, and concentrating budget onto it lifted efficiency across the board. The account is now doing more on $789 a day than it did on $1,221, which is the cleanest possible validation of the post promo plan and of holding budgets steady from here.

A dedicated low budget testing campaign goes up as the home for all new creative. Concepts enter in their own ABO ad set, run against soft metric and efficiency gates, and only graduate into the engines once they prove out. This protects the stabilising TLIMS and Value Opt signal from disruption while still surfacing the next winners, and it gives a clean weekly feedback report on which angles and hooks are taking. It is the structure that lets us raise creative velocity without destroying performance every cycle.

Weekly creative cadence, the velocity shift

Moving from a two week creative cycle to a weekly Friday refresh batch, in line with the velocity and diversity direction from the Meta Performance Summit. The aim is 10 to 15 iterations a week once the design capacity is in place, built as structured variations on proven winners rather than random volume, statics first since that is what this account converts on. Sent ahead of the Thursday call for review, turned on Friday. This is how we feed sharper test reads and keep the account ahead of creative fatigue.
