The recovery is no longer a question of efficiency on shrinking spend. What's Not Working

One Structural Drag, One Funnel Constraint Deepening, One Operational Cleanup

What
Evergreen Christian Art CBO recorded $0 attributed revenue on $1,409 of spend this week, the third consecutive week of zero attribution. Cumulative three-week Evergreen spend now stands at $4,168 against $1,667 of attributed revenue, all of which landed in the May 7 to 13 window only. Christian Collection Grid Static took $893 of this week's spend with 32 ATCs and 6 ICs and zero attributed purchases. The previously proven Apr 26 Abide With Me Narrative Video also recorded $0 on $426 of spend.
Why
Three weeks of consistent funnel signal (CTR holding above 2%, ATCs accumulating, ICs registering) at zero attributed purchases points to one of two things, neither of which is a creative problem. Either the pixel is mis-firing on the Christian Collection landing page that Evergreen ads route to, or the audience the Evergreen CBO surfaces is reaching the PDP and abandoning at the checkout step (which would corroborate the account-wide IC to Purchase decline from 30.9% to 26.2% this week, and the PDP friction Oscar flagged on the Thursday call). The Triple Whale read should be diagnostic, if Triple Whale shows revenue against Evergreen ads that Meta does not, the issue is pixel attribution, if Triple Whale also shows zero, the issue is checkout drop-off and routes back to the PDP work already in motion.
Fix
Avi cross-check this week, target a clean read by Wednesday May 27 (taking into account UK bank holiday Monday May 26). If pixel issue, fix at source and revisit Evergreen budget allocation. If checkout drop-off, pause Evergreen campaign in full, reallocate $200/day budget to TLIMS ABO and Combined 50/50 ad sets which are returning 2.6x or better, revisit Evergreen as a structural decision in the post Memorial Day consolidation review (slide 7).
What
Account-wide IC to Purchase rate dropped from 30.9% to 26.2%, ATC to IC fell from 28.6% to 21.3%, both for the second consecutive week. The pattern is now confirmed across two reporting periods, not a one-week noise event. 376 ATCs and 80 ICs across the account, only 21 purchases landed.
Why
Click to ATC went from 5.2% to 9.1% (a 75% lift), Meta is now pulling materially better qualified clicks into the funnel. The leak has moved one step further down. Higher AOV explains part of it, $1,010 average order means more cart deliberation, but the trade-off is steeper than the simple AOV logic would predict. The most likely explanation is the PDP and checkout flow are not built for the price point Meta is now matching the account to. This is the structural fix Oscar flagged on the Thursday call, and the Eden landing page is the first paid media instrument to test against it.
Fix
No paid media lever resolves this directly. Eden landing page asset list ready Tuesday May 26 (post UK bank holiday) for Maddy and the engineering team. Once the BOGO concludes, route 15 to 20% of Eden Collection traffic to the new landing page in a controlled A/B against the current Eden PDP. If lift confirms, expand to TLIMS and additional best sellers. Pricing test work on Oscar's side runs parallel.
What
Eight Memorial Day units inside the new ad set absorbed sub-$5 spend each with no ATC signal across the first five days of trading. Last Supper Anchor Static ($3.15), Best Sellers Detail Video ($3.14), With The Father Three In One Static ($1.90), Christian Mix BOGO Carousel ($1.02), TLIMS Premium Static ($0.95), Fine Art Both Rooms Video ($0.86), Peace Be Still Details Video ($0.54), Christian Collection Art Defines Video ($0.10).
Why
Meta is correctly concentrating budget on the two carriers, With The Father Stillness Static (18.65x) and Christian Collection BOGO Static (3.11x), which is exactly how a tight ad set should function during a high-stakes promotional window. The eight non-delivering units never received enough audience exposure to evaluate, and continuing to leave them in the rotation creates the risk that Meta starts feeding them as the proven carriers saturate, which would dilute signal at the worst possible moment.
Fix
All eight paused at 16:17 hrs Tuesday May 19 per the Memorial Day optimisation checklist, action already complete. Ad set now runs on three active units (Stillness, BOGO, Abide With Me BOGO) which preserves creative diversity and the proven carrier signal. Memorial Day budget lift to $125/day (also Tuesday) gives the two carriers more delivery room, second step lift to $156 conditional on Wednesday May 21 read holding 5x+ Meta ROAS.
