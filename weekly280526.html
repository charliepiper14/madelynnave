The Account Cleared Its Target, This Is The Week The Recovery Became A Scale Story

Five straight weeks of improvement and the account did not just touch the 3.5x profitability target this week, it cleared it at 6.38x with revenue almost tripling week on week. Spend rose 16% to $1,352 a day while CPA dropped to $163, less than half the historical breakeven, so the gains arrived on more volume rather than less. Purchases nearly tripled from 21 to 58. The Memorial Day BOGO did the heavy lifting, but the underlying account is also stronger, every one of the four campaigns returned above 4.6x for the first time since onboarding. The story has shifted from how to recover to how to hold this elevated base as the promo winds down.

The Checkout Constraint From The Last Two Weeks Has Resolved

The mid funnel leak that defined the last two reporting periods has reversed. ATC to IC climbed from 21.3% to 37.2% and IC to Purchase from 26.3% to 32.0%, both sharp single week recoveries. The BOGO offer plus stronger creative pulled buyers cleanly through the cart and checkout steps that were previously stalling. This matters because it confirms the leak was promotional and structural rather than permanent, and it de risks the read going into June. The Eden landing page work still has a role, but the urgency drops a level now the funnel is converting at full strength again. Detail on slide 6.

The Two Week Mid-Funnel Drop Has Fully Reversed

The constraint that defined the last two decks has closed. ATC to IC jumped from 21.3% to 37.2% and IC to Purchase from 26.3% to 32.0%, with 487 ATCs converting to 181 ICs to 58 purchases. The leak was sitting between cart and checkout, and that is exactly the step that recovered most. The BOGO offer removed price hesitation at the decision point and the stronger creative brought through buyers with clearer intent. Lower CTR at higher Click to ATC is the healthy version of this trade, fewer idle clicks and more of them converting, which is why revenue per click rose sharply even as raw click volume eased.

What This Means For The Eden Landing Page Work

The landing page workstream was framed as the fix for the checkout leak. With the funnel now converting at full strength, the landing page shifts from urgent repair to deliberate optimisation. The right move is to build it properly against Eden, the confirmed anchor product, and test it once the promo concludes rather than rushing it live mid promo. The asset list still goes across as planned so Maddy and the engineers can prep, and the A/B runs the week of June 2 when the account returns to a clean non promo baseline that makes the read trustworthy.
