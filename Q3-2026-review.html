What Built Q3

Budget moved to where it earned. The core lanes took 52.9 percent of spend in July, 78.7 percent in August and 90.2 percent in September, and returned 3.30x across the quarter against 1.04x in the test sandboxes. Every order was worth more. The Meta basket averaged $996 in Q3 against $957 in Q2 and $616 in Q1. The Labor Day offer landed. The promo week returned 7.14x and lifted September to 3.95x, above May's 3.19x and every month before it.

The Store Grew With It

Shopify recorded $686,954 of gross sales in Q3, up 31 percent, and $485,719 of net sales, up 34 percent, on 545 orders. Average order value rose 6 percent to $935 and sessions rose 49 percent. Meta revenue came to 78.5 percent of store net sales, against 71.8 percent in Q2. After all Meta spend, store contribution reached $252,826, 41 percent above Q2.

Where the Week Stands

The weeks without an offer still run below the 3.5x target, and closing that gap is the Q4 job.
