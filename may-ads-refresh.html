Day 7 Decisions and the 17-Day Arc

How each unit gets judged, and where this leads

No ad gets pulled before seven days. After seven days every ad gets read against the same simple criteria. Below threshold, clear decision. Above threshold, scale or compound.

Outcome at day 7 Decision Rationale
Above 3.5x ROAS Scale, build duplicate in sister ad set, replicate format This is the super-ad signal. Compound the find.
