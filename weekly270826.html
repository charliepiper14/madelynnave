The same ad, pointing at the same page, across 3 consecutive weeks. Basket $1,231.67, then $1,577.91, then $584.83. Orders went 4, 4, 5 and spend rose each week. Why This Does Not Change The Plan

Breakeven on this week's $804.92 basket is around $644 an order. We bought at $398.97, so every order still carries roughly $245 of contribution and the account stays profitable at a lower basket. If basket recovers toward the month to date $957.34 on this week's cost per order, the blended figure reads about 2.4x on identical media. The media is not the thing to fix here.

What I Would Like To Check

Whether anything changed on site during the week, particularly with the Shopify Plus checkout and the new product pages going live. If the mix has really moved toward smaller pieces then the media plan is unchanged and we simply report a lower basket. If it is a page change, it is worth knowing before we read another week of data through it.
