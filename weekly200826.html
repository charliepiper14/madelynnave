What Happened

Mosaic 17 was killed at $1,224.30 with 2 orders and a 1.44x return, on the basket qualifier, because its first order came in 41 percent below our average. In the days after the pause it credited 2 further orders worth $1,768.00 at an $884 basket. Its combined position is now $1,623.10 for 4 orders at 2.18x, which is profitable at a $406 cost per order. On the numbers we had at the time the call was right. On the numbers we have now it was early.

The Adjustment I Would Make

The gate should read on spend and orders that are at least 7 days settled, not on the current window. In practice that means an ad crossing a threshold today gets its verdict next Thursday against the same window, so late credit has landed before the decision. It costs a few days of spend on ads that are already dead and it stops us cutting ads whose orders are still in flight. Homeward is the control here, killed the same day and still at 0.41x combined, so the rule caught the right one.
