If he asks

Has the restructure worked?
Early, but the direction is right. The two days before it were the worst of the month at 0.80x. The four days since are at 1.86x with the window still open, and Proven Scale is running 4.28x. I would give it until next Thursday for a clean read.
Why does Ads Manager show me 1.49x?
A 7-day view starts on the 5th, so it averages the two bad days before the change with the four better ones after. Split at the 8th and it reads 0.80x before and 1.86x after.
You closed General Christian and it did 5.93x in its last days.
On $337 of spend and two orders, and those two were the only orders the whole account recorded on the 6th and 7th. Across the full 11 days it ran 1.63x and across 30 days 1.91x, well under what we kept. Its two best assets are already working inside Proven Scale.
Is the new batch working?
No orders in four days, which is what I would expect at a $478 cost per order. The number I am watching is carts to checkouts.
Should we spend more?
Not this week. We are at $1,495 a day against the $1,500 ceiling and 46 percent of it sits in testing. The gain available costs nothing: move money out of nine thin test cells into three real ones.
What is your best ad?
4 Partitioned Lifestyle in the recycling lane, $624.51 for five orders at $124.90 each across the 11 days. Since the restructure it is Designed By Madelynn Ave at 5.19x on $373.21 with an 8.11 percent click rate.
