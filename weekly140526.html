Why This Is The Best Week Of The Working Relationship

This is the first time the account has cleared the revenue profitability threshold since onboarding. ROAS at 2.15x is materially above the 1.24x revenue ROAS breakeven at this week's $977 AOV (calculated against the unit economics in the intake brief). Revenue grew 25% week on week while spend fell 7%, meaning every dollar that entered the account worked 34% harder than it did the week before. The recovery trajectory of the past three weeks (1.27x to 1.22x to 1.60x to 2.15x) has now become a clear upward run, and the account is operating with $395/day of unused headroom against the $1,500 daily ceiling.

The AOV Story, And Why CPA Is Less Useful This Week

17 purchases generated $16,605 of revenue, an AOV of $977 against the historical 90-day average of $560 to $620. Six of those purchases came through Feb '26 Abide With Me at $1,209 average order. Several other ads delivered single $1,300+ orders. Customers buying higher up the price ladder is the underlying signal, and it changes the framing on CPA. The $424 CPA breakeven was set against a $560 AOV. Applying the same unit economics from the intake brief to a $977 AOV gives a breakeven CPA closer to $788, which means the $455 CPA this week is comfortably profitable on a revenue basis even though it sits above the historical CPA target.

What's Not Working

Three Drag Points, And Why The Action Plan Holds Until Friday

What
Christian Collection Grid Static absorbed $983 of Evergreen budget at 0.65x with 1 purchase. Launched May 9 inside the Evergreen Christian Art CBO, this ad took the majority of the campaign's weekly spend after Sunday's strong 1.52x early read flagged in the start-of-week update. It has since softened across the back of the week as ATC volume held (12 ATCs) but IC and purchase conversion dropped.
Why
This is the new creative still inside its 7-day evaluation hold, and Meta concentrated budget on it because the early signal was strong before the audience saturated. The Christian Collection Grid format is also competing inside Evergreen against the Apr '26 Abide With Me Narrative Video which has been running successfully for four weeks. The newer ad got the budget, the older ad delivered the conversions at a higher ROAS. The Grid Static may yet stabilise above 1x once the audience widens past the early adopters.
Fix
Hold until the 7-day evaluation window closes on Friday May 16, in line with the protocol agreed in the May 9 Loom walkthrough. If Christian Collection Grid Static has not crossed 1x by Friday, redirect Evergreen budget back onto Abide With Me Narrative Video and queue the Christian Collection Narrative Video (currently at $10 spend) for a structured retest with an isolated ad set.
What
Fine Art Carousel softened from 1.78x to 0.99x and now sits on $1,470 spend with 1 purchase inside Fine Art ABO Value Opt. This was last week's validated new creative and the only ad in Fine Art ABO carrying meaningful spend. The campaign overall fell to 0.91x as a result.
Why
Post-novelty fatigue setting in two weeks after launch. The carousel format works for fine art (confirmed by the 1.78x read last week) but the same creative cannot carry a campaign indefinitely without rotation. Stillness Video and Carousel 2 Fine Art are both sitting inside the ABO at sub-$50 spend, which means Meta is not feeding them and the carousel is taking the full budget alone. The Feb '26 Grid duplicate that was meant to be paused per last week's plan is also still showing $82 of spend with no conversions, which suggests the pause action either did not land or was reverted.
Fix
Confirm the Feb '26 Grid duplicate inside Fine Art ABO Value Opt is paused (it should not be receiving spend, the winning version sits in Combined Test). Pause Carousel 2 Fine Art inside Combined Test where it has spent $72 in the week with no engagement. The second fine art carousel currently in development with Oscar lands this week and goes into the Fine Art ABO Value Opt ad set as the next rotation. This buys time for the original Fine Art Carousel to either stabilise or be retired in favour of the new creative.
What
Seven new and dormant TLIMS CBO ads are sitting in sub-$30 delivery with zero purchases between them. With The Father Static, TLIMS Reviews Static, With The Father Narrative Video, Christian Mix Close Up Carousel, plus three Brand Logo statics from the February batch, collectively spent $70 in the seven days with no conversions.
Why
Two things are happening. The first is that Meta is correctly choosing not to feed these ads while Feb '26 Abide With Me is delivering 6.74x in the same ad set, which is exactly how budget concentration is meant to work. The second is the 7-day evaluation hold agreed on the May 9 Loom walkthrough, which protects all launch creative from action until Friday May 16. This is not a failure of these ads, it is a structural pause window. The Brand Logo statics from February however are a different category and have been underdelivering for several weeks.
Fix
Hold all four May 9 launches inside TLIMS CBO until Friday May 16 in line with the launch protocol. Pause the three Brand Logo statics from February (TLIMS, Good Shepherd, Abide With Me) which have had ample evaluation time and continue to absorb micro-spend with no funnel signal. Memorial Day BOGO ads received from Oscar today (May 14) will be built into a new dedicated ad set inside TLIMS CBO ready for the Saturday May 16 launch, which gives the campaign a clean refresh moment.

Wasted Spend Has Collapsed Compared To Three Weeks Ago

Zero-purchase ads consumed approximately $740 across 21 ads this week, or 9.6% of total spend. Three weeks ago this figure was running closer to $2,000 to $2,500 per week. The bulk of the remaining waste is Eden Heirloom Video C1 at $347 (already paused mid-week, residual spend before the pause took full effect) and the Feb '26 Grid duplicate at $82 which should not be receiving any spend at all. The fixes above remove a further ~$500 to $700 per week from the waste line, which can be redirected to Feb '26 Abide With Me and the Memorial Day BOGO push.

Top Of Funnel Strengthened, Click Quality Up A Third

Click to ATC moved from 3.9% to 5.2%, a 33% lift. This is the clearest evidence that the creative concentration is putting better-qualified clicks into the funnel. Where the account was paying for impressions and clicks that did not translate into product page engagement, this week's traffic is engaging with products at a meaningfully higher rate. Feb '26 Abide With Me alone generated 43 ATCs from its $1,075 of spend, which is a 5.7% click to ATC rate and well above the account average. The implication for scaling: the audience pools surfacing this engagement quality have spare depth, and Combined Test plus TLIMS CBO Value Opt are both candidates for measured budget lift.

ATC To IC Tightened, But The Reason Is Mix Not Health

ATC to IC dropped from 36.3% to 28.6%. On its own this would be a warning, but the context softens it. AOV jumped from $633 to $977 in the same window, which means more shoppers are putting higher-value items in their cart and a portion of those are taking longer to commit. The IC to purchase rate held at 30.9% (vs 31.8%), so once shoppers reach checkout they are still closing at the same rate. This is the higher-AOV trade-off in action: fewer but bigger orders, slightly more friction at the ATC to IC step, and overall revenue and ROAS materially up. The Klaviyo welcome flow recovery noted in last week's deck appears to be holding, and the close rate is no longer the constraint it was three weeks ago.
