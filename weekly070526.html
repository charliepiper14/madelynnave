Why This Is The Turning Point

Spend dropped 28% week on week as the budget reductions and pauses applied April 24 to 28 took full effect. Revenue dropped only 5% because the budget that remained landed on ads that actually convert. The result is more purchases (21 from 16) on materially less spend, with CPA falling 45% from $719 to $396. This week's CPA is the first week below the $424 breakeven for several months. ROAS fractionally below the 1.61x ROAS breakeven at $633 AOV, but the trajectory is unambiguous.

The Funnel Is Healing

Two close-rate metrics jumped this week. ATC to IC moved from 25.0% to 36.3% account-wide, and IC to purchase moved from 28.6% to 31.8%. This is the welcome flow recovery showing up in the numbers. Klaviyo was off for 10 days through April, and Oscar confirmed it was reactivated late last week. The expected 7 to 10 day recovery window has played out exactly as predicted in the May 3 update. The funnel that converts ad clicks into orders is back online.

Combined Test Has Become The Best Campaign In The Account

The Combined Test went from 0.68x to 3.49x in a single week. Feb '26 Grid took $1,583 spend and returned $5,790 across 9 purchases, a 3.66x. The earlier Carousel 2 Fine Art that was dragging this campaign last week ($1,501 spend at 0.94x) was effectively de-prioritised by Meta down to $75 spend this week. The campaign now also leads on conversion quality, with a 51% ATC to IC rate and 41% IC to purchase, both materially ahead of the rest of the account. Per Oscar's note that this campaign is currently running only fine art ads, the takeaway is that Feb '26 Grid is the strongest fine art creative in the catalogue and the Combined Test budget should be protected and expanded.

TLIMS CBO Looks Like A Step Back, But The Detail Tells A Different Story

The headline drop from 1.90x to 1.10x in TLIMS CBO is driven almost entirely by one ad. Mar '26 Spring Eden Heirloom Video C1 was last week's hero at 4.06x and dropped to 0.43x this week on $1,345 of spend. That single ad lost approximately $760 of revenue versus its prior week pace. Strip Eden Heirloom out of TLIMS CBO and the rest of the campaign is running at roughly 1.59x. Inside TLIMS CBO, Mother's Day Copy 1 returned 3.61x on $556 spend, the new bright spot. The action this week is replacing Eden Heirloom and rebalancing TLIMS CBO budget toward Mother's Day Copy 1 and the proven Feb Abide With Me.

What's Not Working

Three Drag Points Cost The Account Approximately $1,800 of Spend This Week

What
Eden Heirloom Video C1 fell from 4.06x last week to 0.43x this week. The ad spent $1,345 inside the TLIMS CBO Eden Collection ad set and returned $583 from a single purchase. ATC volume held up reasonably (32 ATCs) but the IC rate collapsed from prior week and only one IC turned into a purchase.
Why
Classic creative fatigue compounded by audience saturation. Eden Heirloom was the hero ad of the spring collection and absorbed concentrated budget for the previous two weeks. Frequency on the Eden Collection ad set has built up, and the audience that responds to this format has now seen the ad multiple times. The funnel signal also shows the issue is not curiosity (ATC still building) but commitment at IC and checkout. The Eden product was recently flagged by Oscar as having only 2 abandoned checkouts in 14 days, which rules out the pixel and product page as the cause.
Fix
Pause Eden Heirloom Video C1 today. Reduce the Eden Collection ad set budget from $200/day to $100/day for the remainder of the week. Replace the lead creative with the Eden Heirloom Static C1 (currently dormant in the same ad set) and queue the next iteration of Eden creative in the May creative round. Consider migrating Eden Heirloom into the Evergreen CBO once the new creative iteration lands, in line with Oscar's seasonal-to-evergreen migration approach.
What
Lunainlayers Partnership ad spent $326 and delivered zero purchases before the ad set was paused mid-week. Last week it spent $1,740 at 0.81x. Decision to wind down was made on the Friday call and confirmed in writing.
Why
The whitelisted creator partnership did not find its footing across the 11-day window it ran. Engagement was good (1,675 ThruPlays this week alone) but the IC rate stayed below the account average. The partnership format itself is not the problem (the account has historically had good results with creator content) but this specific creator's audience overlap with the Madelynn Ave buyer profile was thinner than expected.
Fix
Confirmed paused, no further action. Capture the learning: future partnership tests need a clearer audience match-back to the existing buyer base (interior designers and 35-54 high-end home owners) before launch and a stricter $700 spend cap on the test before evaluating whether to continue.
What
Feb '26 Grid running inside Fine Art ABO Value Opt spent $393 with zero purchases. The same exact creative inside the Combined Test returned 3.66x on $1,583. Same ad, same week, two campaigns, completely opposite outcomes.
Why
The Fine Art ABO version is being out-competed by the Fine Art Carousel inside the same ad set. The CBO algorithm in the Combined Test had different audience signal to feed Feb Grid, and that audience converted. Inside the dedicated Fine Art ABO, the audience landed on the Carousel first and the Grid was a secondary touch that did not close.
Fix
Pause the Feb '26 Grid duplicate inside Fine Art ABO Value Opt today. Concentrate the Fine Art ABO budget on the Fine Art Carousel (1.78x) and Stillness Video as it exits learning. The Combined Test version of Feb Grid stays untouched and continues to receive the bulk of its budget there. This is not a creative failure, it is a duplicate placement issue and the fix is removing the duplicate.

Combined Drag, Approximately $1,800 of Wasted Spend Across These Three Issues This Week

Eden Heirloom $1,345, Lunainlayers $326, and Feb Grid Fine Art duplicate $393 sum to roughly $2,065. After Eden Heirloom budget reallocation rather than pure cut, the recoverable spend per week is closer to $1,300 to $1,500 redirected toward proven performers. Applied to ads currently sitting at 3.5x, this is approximately $4,500 to $5,000 of additional weekly revenue at no additional spend.
