Cart creation thinned, link CTR eased to 1.79% and the click to cart rate fell to 5.6%, which is the demand and creative freshness softening showing up at the top of the funnel. Final completion settled to 24.4% off last week's elevated 36.6%, and with the recent days inside the attribution window that rate will firm up. Combined with a lower $871 basket, the net is softer revenue, driven by fewer and smaller orders rather than a checkout breakdown.
