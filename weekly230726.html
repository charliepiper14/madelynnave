The One Number That Explains The Week

Three test campaigns took $4,237 and returned nothing, and a fourth took $1,833 and returned $627. That is $6,070, 49 percent of all spend, returning 0.10x. In the same week the two proven carriers, Fine Art Value Opt and Evergreen, took just $2,138, 17 percent of spend, and returned $6,830 at 3.20x. Had that drag budget sat on the carriers at their proven efficiency, the week reads 2.63x. The selling engine did not fail, it was underfunded while unproven lanes were overfunded.

The Genuine Positives, Demand Is At Its Strongest All Year

Every top of funnel signal hit a record. Link CTR rose to 2.58 percent, the highest of the entire reported run through 1.97, 2.08, 2.53 and now 2.58. Landing page views climbed 55 percent to 11,211 and impressions reached 455,583. The advertorial cold ads went live and their hooks are elite immediately, 5.03 percent and 3.69 percent. The winners kept winning, Eden Faith Design at 6.42x, Abide Versatile at 5.81x, the Refresh Eden static at 5.04x and the Fine Art Grid at 3.27x.

The Honest Read, This Is The Weakest Week Since The Post Promo Trough

I am not going to dress it up. At 1.12x on 15 orders this is the softest week since early June, and spend ran at $1,773 a day against our $1,500 ceiling for a third week. Two sandboxes have now cooled for three weeks, the Fine Art Refresh at zero this week and the Christian Refresh at 0.34x. The good news is that every one of those causes is a decision we control, not a market we cannot, and each has a specific correction on the plan slide.

Owning The Gap Plainly

That is a discipline failure on allocation, not a market problem, and it is mine to fix. The daily guardrail I committed to last week, checking frequency and cost per purchase at ad set level each morning and pausing at ad level before budget burns, is the mechanism that stops this recurring, and it is now backed by a hard cap on test share.
